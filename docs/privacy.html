Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

College Expense Tracker is a personal, self-hosted app run by its owner for their own use.

  • What it accesses: only the owner's own Google Sheet (to read the table and append expense rows) and a single Google Drive folder the app creates (drive.file scope, so it cannot see any other Drive file).
  • What it stores: receipt photos in that Drive folder and expense rows in that Sheet. Both stay in the owner's Google account. The OAuth refresh token and client secret live only on the owner's own machines.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. There is no analytics or tracking. Receipt files are viewable by anyone who has their link; the Sheet and folder are not made public.
  • Retention and deletion: data stays until the owner deletes it from Google Drive or Sheets. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Google API data: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
