College Expense Tracker

College Expense Tracker is a personal, self-hosted app for logging grocery receipts. The owner photographs a receipt on their phone, reviews the date and total read by on-device OCR, and saves it as a new row in their own Google Sheet, with the photo stored in their own Google Drive.

The app is not offered to the public and has no accounts other than its owner's. It requests Google Sheets access and Google Drive access limited to files it creates (drive.file).
